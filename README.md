## About Me <sub>关于我</sub>

I'm Jason, a 21-year-old developer based in Nanjing, China, and a graduate of Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. My work focuses on **agent harnesses and runtimes, execution security, and cross-platform engineering**.

> **Mission:** Eliminate silent failures across the boundaries of AI agent systems — in code, paths, streams, state, and credentials.

I contribute deeply to high-impact open-source projects, with more than **30,000 lines merged** across design and bug-fix contributions. I'm a preselected intern for the **2026 Apache Casbin Rising Star** program and an organization member of [openagent](https://github.com/the-open-agent/openagent) and [loopx](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx). Articles I wrote have reached **#1 site-wide on V2EX, Zhihu, and Nowcoder**. I have also worked at a top-VC-backed startup and an early-stage company, independently building an enterprise AI product from zero to one and attracting interest from multiple institutions.

I hope to keep learning, become a developer whose work is trusted, and build something that meaningfully improves how people work.
